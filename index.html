Privacy Notice

Home Music Assistant

Purpose

Home Music Assistant is a privately operated integration connecting Amazon Alexa devices with a self-hosted Music Assistant server.

Information Used

The integration may process Amazon account authorization tokens, Alexa device identifiers, playback requests, and music playback metadata to provide its functionality.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the Amazon account, communicate with compatible Alexa devices, and provide music playback and control functionality.

Data Sharing

This personal installation is not intended to sell or advertise using personal information. Data may pass between Amazon, Home Assistant, and Music Assistant as necessary to provide the service.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and tokens may be stored locally by the self-hosted Home Assistant integration. Access is restricted to the system administrator.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the administrator of this private Home Assistant installation.
